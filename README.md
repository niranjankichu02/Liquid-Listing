💧 Liquid Listings
Hackathon Useless Project 3.0

Welcome to Liquid Listings, a revolutionary real estate platform dedicated to generating 100% unstable, highly unlivable blueprints for houses constructed entirely from fluid food items. Why build with concrete when you can build with Rasam?

🚀 Key Features
Dynamic Fluid Architecture: Choose from a wide menu of liquids (from Sambar to Chai) or upload a picture to "scan" your building materials.

Viscosity Physics: A completely inaccurate slider that calculates structural durability based on how thick your fluid is.

Weather Simulation: Toggle between sunny, rainy, or freezing conditions to instantly calculate your mansion's evaporation or flooding timeline.

Live Pest Tracking: Get generated updates on which local wildlife (stray dogs, pigeons, or mosquitoes) is currently drinking your walls.

🛠️ Tech Stack
Frontend: HTML5 and CSS3 featuring a custom Glassmorphism frosted UI.

Logic Engine: Vanilla JavaScript to handle the math, weather impacts, and random state generation.

Architecture: Zero dependencies, single-file structure, and fully responsive design.
